# Recording scripts

Record each take in one go, 25 to 30 seconds, in a quiet room with the phone about 15 cm from your mouth. The clone only needs the best 10 to 14 seconds; the extra lets `prepare_ref.py` cut at a clean pause. Swap in your own name and city.

Save as `recordings/me_en_ad`, `me_en_calm`, `me_hi_ad`, `me_hi_calm`, `me_ta_ad`, `me_ta_calm` (m4a, mp3 or wav).

## English

**Ad energy:** Hey, this is [name] from [city]. You know what I love? Building things with AI and actually shipping them. If you want to learn how we do it, it's free. No fees, no fake gurus. Just DM us, and let's build something real together.

**Calm:** Let me tell you how this started. One phone, a small town, and a lot of curiosity. I taught myself, then taught my friends, then strangers at free workshops. If a small-town kid can do it, honestly, so can you. Start small, and keep going.

## Hinglish

**Ad energy:** Hey dosto, main hoon [name]. Seedhi baat bolun? AI seekhna mushkil nahi hai, bas shuru karna padta hai. Humne ek chhote se town se start kiya, aur aaj kai log humare saath build kar rahe hain. DM karo, baaki main sambhal lunga.

**Calm:** Ek baat sach bataun? Jab pehli baar maine apne school ke teachers ko AI sikhaya, mujhe laga main sapna dekh raha hoon. Paisa us din kuch nahi mila, lekin jo izzat mili, woh kisi bhi fees se badi thi.

## Tanglish

**Ad energy:** Hey makkale, naan [name]. Straight-a sollanum-na, AI kathukradhu kashtam illa, start pannanum, adhu dhaan. Chinna ooru-la irundhu start panninom, ippo neraya builders namma kooda irukkanga. DM pannunga, meedhi naan paathukaren.

**Calm:** Oru vishayam unmaiya sollanum. Naan padicha school-ku poi, enakku paadam eduthavangalukke AI solli kuduthen. Andha naal kaasu edhuvum varala. Aana avanga sonna oru vaarthai, endha fees-a vida perusu.

Tip: Whisper struggles with romanised Tanglish. After `prepare_ref.py`, rewrite `refs/me_ta_*.txt` in Tamil script with exactly the words you said.
